Harvest Hope Community Impact Grant — 2026 Cycle · Hartwell Family Foundation

The Hartwell Family Foundation is committed to strengthening the fabric of New England communities by investing in nonprofits that address root causes of poverty, hunger, and inequity. The Harvest Hope Community Impact Grant supports organizations delivering direct services to vulnerable populations — with a focus on food access, community health, and youth empowerment.

We prioritize organizations that are deeply embedded in their communities, demonstrate strong fiscal stewardship, and can show measurable outcomes. Grants of up to $50,000 are available for one-year program support or capacity-building initiatives.

Eligibility: 501(c)(3) organizations operating in Massachusetts or Rhode Island with annual budgets under $5 million. Organizations must have been in operation for at least three years.
