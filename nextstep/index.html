The NextStep Youth Economic Opportunity Grant

NextStep Youth Economic Opportunity Grant — 2026 Cycle · Calloway Brothers Family Foundation

The Calloway Brothers Family Foundation was established in 2003 by two first-generation college graduates who grew up in working-class Massachusetts communities. Guided by the belief that economic mobility begins with access to real opportunity, the Foundation invests in programs that connect young people — particularly those from low-income backgrounds — to meaningful work experience, skills training, and pathways to financial independence.

The NextStep Youth Economic Opportunity Grant supports nonprofits delivering workforce readiness, vocational training, paid internship, or career mentorship programs to youth ages 14–24 in underserved communities across Massachusetts. We are especially interested in programs that offer stipends or wages directly to participants, integrate real-world work experience, and demonstrate strong outcomes in employment or post-secondary enrollment.

Grants of up to $25,000 are available for one-year program support. We do not fund general operating costs, capital projects, or endowments.

Eligibility: 501(c)(3) organizations operating in Massachusetts with annual budgets under $3 million. Organizations must have been in operation for at least two years and must serve youth ages 14–24 as a primary population.

Application deadline: September 15, 2026. Late submissions will not be reviewed. Questions? Contact info@callowaybrothers.org.
